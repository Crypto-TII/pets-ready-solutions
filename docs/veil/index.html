VEIL lets AI models compute on encrypted inputs, across cloud and edge. Users keep their data private, and model owners keep their models confidential. It works with FHE, MPC, or both together.

  • Encrypted on your deviceData is encrypted before it leaves the phone or client. The key never leaves it.
  • AI computes on ciphertextThe server runs the model on encrypted data with FHE, MPC, or both. It never sees the input or the result.
  • Only you decryptThe result comes back encrypted. GPU-accelerated inference takes under 0.6 s per token.
